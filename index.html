Río

ha' water

In Maya writing, one sign could hold a whole word. This one reads ha': water. The word is still spoken today.

The water is moving.

Río is a river of light and the sound of a real stream. It runs on its own for as long as you stay.

Listening for the river.

The colors of the water follow the hour where you are.
