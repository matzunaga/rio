# rio
Río: a river of light and the sound of a real stream, running on its own. Tap Begin, and nothing else is ever asked of you.

The water follows the hour where you are: peach and gold at dawn, bright cyan at midday, amber and rose at dusk, deep indigo at night. A touch brings back Pause and an i for the details, then they fade away again; the space bar pauses too. The live flow of the Colorado River at Lees Ferry, from the [USGS Water Data API](https://api.waterdata.usgs.gov/), gently sets the pace of the water; without it the river keeps its own rhythm.

The water sound is "Stream" by gluckose ([Freesound 333987](https://freesound.org/s/333987/)), dedicated to the public domain under CC0, by way of the [Blanket](https://github.com/rafaelmardojai/blanket) app. It is raised 13 dB and saved as MP3, and it loops through a four-second equal-power crossfade. Every tunable number sits at the top of `app.js`; add `?hour=13` to the address to preview any hour.
